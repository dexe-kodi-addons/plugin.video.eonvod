v1.2.0
- Provider resolution follows pvr.eon's rebuilt provider list, and keeps
  reading older pvr.eon installs by their original index meanings.
- Metadata language can now be set to any of the languages EON supports.
- Small fixes.

v1.1.0
- Provider resolution now follows pvr.eon exactly, so accounts on Telemach,
  NetTV Plus, EON Hrvatska, Vivacom and Nova reach their own host.
- Page ids per portal, so the Telemach and Vivacom portal is browsable.
- A sync that returns nothing no longer replaces the stored catalogue.
- Tools stays reachable when the first sync fails.
- Fixed the back history after an empty Favourites folder.

v1.0.0
- First stable release.
- Browses EON On Demand content from the API.
- Uses the same account, session and device data as pvr.eon.
- Movies, series, kids, documentaries, TV shows.
- Caches the catalogue locally for faster browsing.
- Manual and scheduled sync, with progress shown in the corner when scheduled.
- Filters unsubscribed content when configured to do so.
- Age rating filter, with children's titles exempt.
- Resume playback, watched marks, favourites and search history.
- Plays through inputstream.adaptive, with Widevine where required.
